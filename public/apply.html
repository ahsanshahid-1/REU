Apply to the REU

Takes about 25 minutes. There is no fee. You can also apply through NSF ETAP; both routes receive identical consideration. Deadline: February 15, 2027, 11:59 p.m. Central Time.

Eligibility reminder. NSF requires REU participants to be U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, or U.S. permanent residents, enrolled in (or accepted to) an undergraduate degree program. Details on the eligibility page.
