Applying

Apply through NSF ETAP

Applications to this REU are accepted only through NSF's Education & Training Application (ETAP). There is no separate application on this site and no application fee. Dates are tentative and subject to change; the exact application window will be posted here once it is confirmed — applications are expected to open in January 2027.

Eligibility reminder. NSF requires REU participants to be U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, or U.S. permanent residents, enrolled in (or accepted to) an undergraduate degree program. Details are on the eligibility page.
