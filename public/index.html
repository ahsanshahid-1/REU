NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates · Dates to be confirmed

Spend your summer doing real AI research in Little Rock.

This is a fully funded, eight-week, in-residence NSF REU. A cohort of 10 students from institutions across the United States join active faculty research groups for hands-on work in AI, cybersecurity, and data science. It is built for students who might not otherwise get the chance — so no prior experience is required, and there is no application fee, ever.

  • $700 / week stipend
  • Free housing & meals
  • Travel covered
  • No experience needed

What is an REU?

A paid summer to try real research

An REU is a summer research program funded by the National Science Foundation. You join a small cohort, get matched with a faculty mentor, and work on a genuine research project alongside graduate students and other undergrads. Our theme is efficient, secure, and trustworthy AI — think smarter models that run on small devices, defending systems from cyberattacks, and building AI people can actually trust.

Your own project

You do not fetch coffee. You take on a real research question with your mentor and grow into leading it yourself over the summer.

A summer crew

All ten students live together, meet weekly, and share one theme — so you build friendships and a research community, not just a resume line.

Mentors in your corner

Every mentor is trained to support you, and keeps working with you after the summer on recommendation letters and next steps toward grad school.
