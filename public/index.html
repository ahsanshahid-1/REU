Overview

What an NSF REU Site is

The National Science Foundation's Research Experiences for Undergraduates program supports active research participation by undergraduate students in any research area NSF funds. An REU Site engages a cohort of students in meaningful ways in ongoing research programs, with a well defined common focus that enables a shared cohort experience. This Site's common focus is data-driven discovery and emerging technologies: connecting nanoscale materials research with data science, visualization, and secure computing, the areas where UA Little Rock's research centers are strongest.

Meaningful research participation

Each student owns a defined research question inside an active faculty research program. NSF's model moves students from a relatively dependent status to as independent a status as their competence warrants.

A cohort with a common focus

All ten students share housing, a weekly seminar, and a common intellectual theme, building the student-faculty interaction and student-student communication that NSF requires REU Sites to foster.

High quality mentoring

Every research mentor receives training to promote the quality of students' research and to reinforce NSF's expectations for positive, professional mentor-student interactions.
