Applying

Applications are handled by NSF ETAP

This program no longer uses a separate account or application form on this website. All applications are submitted through NSF's Education & Training Application (ETAP), where you create your account and track your status. Dates are tentative and subject to change; applications are expected to open in January 2027, and the exact window will be posted here.
